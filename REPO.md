```#meta/library/remote
name: Silverware Toolbar
description: A toolbar by the text you select (docked over the keyboard on a phone) to format it, turn lines into lists, headings, quotes or code blocks, and run any action or command, with gesture buttons, text snippets, and icons and emoji of your own (with Silverware Icons).
author: damesene
website: https://github.com/damesene/silverware-libraries/tree/main/silverware-toolbar
uri: https://github.com/damesene/silverware-libraries/blob/main/silverware-toolbar/silverware-toolbar.md
```

```#meta/library/remote
name: Silverware Tags
description: "Manage the tags of your space: rename and merge them everywhere (pages, items, tasks), remove them (or only their #), tag many pages at once found by folder or by their tags, clean up tags likely the same, a Tags panel in the navigator; every change reviewed first and undoable."
author: damesene
website: https://github.com/damesene/silverware-libraries/tree/main/silverware-tags
uri: https://github.com/damesene/silverware-libraries/blob/main/silverware-tags/silverware-tags.md
```

```#meta/library/remote
name: Silverware Chart
description: "Charts written as Mermaid (so other Mermaid renderers can draw them too), drawn in SilverBullet with Chart.js: bar, line, area, pie, doughnut, radar and more, colors from your theme; their data from the block or from any markdown table of the page (above, below, under a heading, or one named in Silverware Table), only some of its rows if you like, the chart following the table as you edit it."
author: damesene
website: https://github.com/damesene/silverware-libraries/tree/main/silverware-chart
uri: https://github.com/damesene/silverware-libraries/blob/main/silverware-chart/silverware-chart.md
```
