# Silverware Assets DOCX: changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-14)

First release.

- With Silverware Assets, a Word document's (.docx) preview is its first page, drawn smaller.
- A document's preview is made once and kept in your space (with Silverware Assets), shown from there on every device.
- A document shown full, in the side panel or enlarged is a reader of the whole document, with the page number and zoom.
- SilverBullet's shortcuts pressed in the reader do nothing there and say so.
- docx-preview comes with the library: it works offline.
