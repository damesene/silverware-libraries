# Silverware Assets XLSX: changelog

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-19)

- The document reader scrolls with a thin scrollbar, the same in every Silverware plugin; SilverBullet's own scrollbar is unchanged.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-17)

- A sheet keeps a spreadsheet's look in SilverBullet's next version (3.0), whose own styling of a table in an embed (striped rows, bold header, padding) no longer shows over it.
- A workbook's preview takes its corners and outline from your theme, like other files' previews, in the page, in a grid and in a grid cell's field alike (with Silverware Assets 1.0.1).

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-14)

First release.

- With Silverware Assets, an Excel workbook's (.xlsx) preview is its first sheet's first cells.
- A workbook's preview is made once and kept in your space (with Silverware Assets), shown from there on every device.
- A workbook shown full, in the side panel or enlarged is a reader of every sheet as a spreadsheet shows it (fonts, colours, borders, merged cells, frozen rows and columns), with a tab for each sheet and zoom.
- SilverBullet's shortcuts pressed in the reader do nothing there and say so.
- ExcelJS comes with the library: it works offline.
