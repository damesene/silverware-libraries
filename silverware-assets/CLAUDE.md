# Silverware Assets

The files a page embeds and links (prefix `swa-`, settings and Space Lua API under `silverware.assets`): each shown full, as a preview or as a card (the views `full`, `preview`, `card`: one name in the interface, the markdown, the settings and the API, docs/silverware-assets-names.md), its menu (the "…", a small arrow after a card's name or a link's text: a type's icon is a picture alone, since 2026-11-16, the user's choice: an icon opening the menu was found by nobody new), a link turned into an embed and back, a file shown in a side panel or enlarged; the settings window (`Silverware Assets: Settings`: tabs Files, Appearance (Preview: a row for all files and one per type, one open, a type's own marked; Full; Card, Link; each on a sample: `ui/labeleditor.ts`, `ui/cardeditor.ts` from the parts of `ui/sample.ts`) from `ui/mediatab.ts`, one set of settings saved once by the first; Markdown). Split out of Silverware Layout 2026-11-14 ([docs/silverware-assets-split.md](../docs/silverware-assets-split.md)); its design and decisions: [docs/silverware-assets.md](../docs/silverware-assets.md). What is left before the branch's merge, and how to work: [docs/silverware-layout-assets-next.md](../docs/silverware-layout-assets-next.md). User docs: `silverware-assets.md`; the code pages `-init.md` (settings, the bridge, the command), `-api.md` (priority 50: `silverware.assets.api`, types and actions registered, `shared/assets.ts`), `-style.md`.

## Mental model

- **Silverware Layout is optional**: its API (`silverware.layout.api`, handed over by the init page) gives a medium's width by its handle (a `LayoutProvider`, as Silverware Table registers) and what its grids draw outside the editor (`shared/rendered.ts`: each file and link drawn, its place in the page). Without it, files are decorated where SilverBullet draws them, without the handle; there are no grids then.
- The code keeps the word "asset" (`shared/assets.ts`: `Asset`, `AssetType`, `AssetAction`), the plugin's name.
- **Layers** (src/isolation.test.ts): `model/` (settings, pure), `core/` (the registry, which type, view and action apply, an embed found in the text), `builtin/` (its own types and actions, through the contract alone: `shared/assets.ts`, and the shared contracts of panels and elements, `sheetarea.ts`, `sidepanel.ts`, `overwindow.ts`, `elements.ts`), `ui/` (card, menus, the settings window's tabs), `decorate.ts` (`AssetDecorator` over an `AssetHost`), `host/silverbullet/` (SilverBullet's embeds and links, its platform, its markdown-as-written elements) used by `index.ts` alone.
- **Which embed a widget of the editor is** (`core/locate.ts`, SilverBullet's widget carries no position, 2.12.1): first where SilverBullet's editor says it is (`positionOf`, **SILVERBULLET-INTERNAL**: `host/silverbullet/editorposition.ts`, `client.editorView.posAtDOM`, the one exception to the rule of no SilverBullet internals, the user's choice 2026-11-14; checked against the text, the isolation test keeps it to that file, `assets.spec.ts` checks it by name; to take it out, delete that file and its one line in `host/silverbullet/assets.ts`), else by the text drawn around it (`contextOf`: the editor's lines before and after it, matched with the written ones less their marks, `closestContext`), else by the run of widgets drawn (`placeAmong`: the editor draws what is in view) and where it is in the page. Located again as a change is written (`locateNow`): another embed may have changed since. Files in a grid have their place, told (`shared/rendered.ts`). **In a grid's cell edited** (Silverware Editor's field) and a database's peek a file is drawn by `editorEmbed`, a link to one given its icon by `editorLinkIcon` and its menu's arrow after its text by `editorLinkMarker` (the editor's `linkAfter`) (both by `fieldDrawing(page)`: registered with Layout's `rendered.drawInFields`, the API's `fieldDrawing`; docs/silverware-editor-links.md; a link's menu built by `linkMenu` in the page and in a field alike, told how to write), its medium the browser's own (`plainMedium`) in a `.swa-frame`: where the editor gives its markdown to edit (shared/editor.ts `EmbedEdit`, 2026-11-15) its menu and a click's action as in the page, what they change written into the field's text (`render`'s `fieldEdit`), its width and alignment by Layout's handle too (its room the field's line, `blockAround`; docs/silverware-layout-room-handles.md), an Alt+press the editor's; else read-only. A popup opened from a field's shadow root is the editor's companion, and over a sheet it opens over it (shared/popupmenu.ts). Drawn again by the type taking it as the registry changes (`onChange`, a burst waited out), until its widget goes.
- **A preview's label** ([docs/silverware-assets-label.md](../docs/silverware-assets-label.md)): `preview.labels` (all files' and a type's own, whole; a full file's alike, `full.labels`), `ui/label.ts` (its texts: the alt text falling back to the name, left out where the name is shown too) drawn by `decorate.ts` into the frame (`.swa-label-above` / `-below`, `data-swa-label-above` moving the "…" down), edited by `ui/labeleditor.ts` (one element: a sample preview, its label's lines the properties shown as their values, a name and × over one pointed at or chosen; those not shown a row of chips; `shared/drag.ts`).
- **Alignment is Silverware Layout's** (docs/silverware-layout-align.md): Assets keeps a file's `align` in its embed's metadata (`{ align: right }`) and hands it over as Layout's entry through its provider (`read`, `write`), with its kinds *Preview* and *Full* (`kindOf` by the view; a card is no widget, at the left). Layout gives it meaning, places it, offers it in its handle's menu and its defaults in its dialog; Assets has no alignment of its own (no menu item, no setting, no CSS), and without Layout none is aligned. Previews are Layout's widgets too, placed, never widened (`resizable` false: their grip opens the menu alone).
- **Types of other plugins** (Silverware Assets PDF, DOCX, XLSX: docs/silverware-viewers.md) register through `silverware.assets.api` and replace a built-in one of their name; the registry tells the decorator (`onChange`), which draws the page's files again a moment later (their init page may run after the files were drawn). A preview whose type puts a document's viewer in later gets the layer over it then (decorate.ts, `previewDone`).
- **Previews kept in the space** ([docs/silverware-assets-stored-previews.md](../docs/silverware-assets-stored-previews.md)): a type with `storesPreview` gets `RenderContext.stored` for a file's preview (`core/stored.ts`: `_silverware-assets-previews/<path>.<lastModified>-<size>.<webp|png|html>`, named by the file's version as `getFileMeta` gives it, read for that version alone: a copy keeping an older time is still another; the host's `previewFiles`; **Clean up previews** takes away those of files and versions gone, `orphanedPreviews`); **Regenerate preview** in its menu takes them away and draws it with `anew`; a file deleted from its menu takes its version's previews with it. The folder is the setting `previewFolder` (default `_silverware-assets-previews`, `validatePreviewFolder`: no empty part, none starting with a dot); changed in the settings window, its files are moved before the setting is saved (`movedPreviews`, index.ts `movePreviewFolder`). The setting `storePreviews` (on by default, the Files tab) turns all of it off: no `stored`, no Regenerate preview (deleting and Clean up previews still take away what is kept). The type keeps what it likes and draws it itself.
- **A file's menu** ([docs/silverware-assets-menu.md](../docs/silverware-assets-menu.md), `ui/moremenu.ts`, built in `decorate.ts` for an embed and a link alike): a **Show as** row (link, card, preview, full: `showAs`, a link's from its own menu written as an embed of the view chosen at once, the page writer's `links.toEmbed(…, changes)`), **Rename…** under it ([docs/silverware-assets-rename.md](../docs/silverware-assets-rename.md), `ui/renamedialog.ts`, `core/rename.ts`: the title written where the embed or link is, in the page or a field, then the file renamed by the host's `renameFile`, SilverBullet's `index.renameDocumentCommand`, its links following, its kept previews taken away), then the open actions, then the file's (`AssetAction.group`: `open` by default, `file`; copies first, Copy markdown among them), Delete last.
- **Files inserted** ([docs/silverware-assets-insert.md](../docs/silverware-assets-insert.md)): a paste, a drop or an upload of files taken from SilverBullet (`host/silverbullet/insert.ts`: the document's capture, decided in the event by the settings and the page as last read; an element marked as taking files itself, `shared/takesfiles.ts`, left alone; *File: Upload* replaced by the init page, SilverBullet's own run where `insert.enabled` is off) into the dialog (`ui/insertdialog.ts`: a row per file; what is pure of it `core/insert.ts`), each file drawn as it will be by the decorator's `drawSample` (a `blob:` URL, read-only, no preview kept), written once Insert is pressed, their upload told meanwhile in a panel at the bottom right (`ui/uploadprogress.ts`: a row per file, its state; SilverBullet tells no bytes), put in where the place was as Insert was pressed, moved by what is typed elsewhere meanwhile (`core/insert.ts` `mappedPlace`). An image of the web (its address given by the paste or the drop, `webUrlOf`) may be linked there instead of kept (*Kept* in its row; `![](https://…)`, `webMarkdownOf`), nothing uploaded. A grid's cell's field through Silverware Editor's API (`surfaceOf`, handed over as `editor`). Silverware Toolbar's action *Upload file* (`toolbarActions`, defined by the init page) into the field it is run in (`ActionEditor.field`). Its API's `fieldDrawing` (shared/assets.ts) draws in another plugin's field as `fieldDrawing` does (Silverware Database's peek). **Another plugin's files** (its API's optional members, shared/assets.ts; Silverware Database's property of files, docs/silverware-database-files.md): `drawPreview` (`decorate.ts`: a type's preview in a box, no frame, menu nor click; a type keeping previews or costly drawn from the one kept alone, none made), `showFile` (the side panel's action, `open-panel`, run as a link's file), `insertFiles` (`index.ts` `uploadFiles`: the dialog `nameOnly`, its companion, the files written by `writeChosen`, the write half of `insertChosen`, nothing put in a page), `deleteFiles` (as *Delete file…*, its kept previews with it, nothing asked). A drop's place by `editorposition.ts`' `posAtCoords`.
- **Zoomed with the font** (Silverware Layout's setting `fontZoom.scaleFiles`, docs/silverware-layout-font-size.md *Files zoomed*): the sizes in px it sets written times `shared/contentscale.ts`' scale (`scaledPx`), a full file of its own size and a preview's image zoomed (the style page), the provider's `scales`; a type given its room as at the scale of 1 (`viewportOf`), its own sizes CSS following the scale (Silverware Assets PDF, DOCX, XLSX: `scaledPx`), nothing drawn again as it changes; its "…" and labels a control's and the text's, their font held (SilverBullet gives a widget's buttons the editor's).
- **Settings**: `silverware.assets.<key>` (views, actions, insert, by view `preview`, `full`, `card`, `link` as the Appearance tab, storePreviews, previewFolder; the former `originals`, `anchor`, `heights`, `previews`, `labels`, `originalLabels`, `linkIcons` written as none, so a save takes them out). Actions by their labels: `open-tab`, `open-panel`, `open-enlarged`, `download`, `copy-image`. A drawn file's element is marked `data-swa-type` (its type) and `data-swa-view`, each key its own line of Assets' own block of Library/Config (marker `-- managed-by: silverware-assets`: tables the Configuration Manager would lose); `silverware.assets.syntaxMode.*` in the Configuration Manager's.
- **Markdown as written** (`shared/syntaxmode.ts`): files hidden (`data-swa-syntax-shown`). A click on a link there is Silverware Layout's (moved 2026-11-15, the user's choice: it is the whole page's, not files').

## Tests

- `src/isolation.test.ts`: the layers (model, core, builtin, ui; SilverBullet only in `host/silverbullet/` and `index.ts`), no other plugin's code (Silverware Layout only through its API, `shared/layout.ts` and `shared/rendered.ts`), of Layout's marks only `data-swl-width` in the style page.
- `ui-tests/` against SilverBullet only (`helpers.ts`: a test's pages and files under `UiAssets/<test>/`, Library/Config put back): `assets.spec.ts` (files in a page and in Layout's grid, the settings window, the markdown-as-written mode), `link-touch.spec.ts` and `settings-touch.spec.ts` (the touch project: a link's icon tapped, the settings window over the whole screen).
