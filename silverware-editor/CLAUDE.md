# Silverware Editor

A markdown editor for the other plugins (prefix `swe-`, API under `silverware.editor`), CodeMirror 6 underneath and never shown: the plugins see `TextEditor` (`shared/editor.ts`). The plan, the probe's results and the decisions: [docs/silverware-editor.md](../docs/silverware-editor.md). User docs: `silverware-editor.md`; `-api.md` (priority 50: `silverware.editor.api`), `-init.md` (the space's pages handed over; CodeMirror loaded when idle).

## Mental model

- **The contract is `shared/editor.ts`**: `Editors.create(doc, options)` gives a `TextEditor` (text, selection, a range replaced, read-only, focus, the selection's rectangle, its element; change, focus and blur told; keys of the plugin's own by CodeMirror's names; `preview`: whether it shows the markdown as rendered, so its user draws it in the rendering's look, as Silverware Layout's cell does), `surfaceOf(node)` the `EditingSurface` a node is in (Silverware Toolbar's). `editorsOf(given)` gives the API handed over, or the stand-in: the same editor as a `<textarea>` (`textareaEditor`), so no plugin needs this library.
- **CodeMirror is a file beside the bundle** (`silverware-editor-codemirror.js`, built by `scripts/build.mjs` from `src/vendor.ts`, never imported by the bundle: `src/codemirror.ts` takes its types alone), loaded by `init` when the browser is idle. `create` is synchronous: CodeMirror's editor where it is loaded, the stand-in's until then.
- **The live preview is `src/livepreview.ts`**: decorations made again on every transaction (the editors are small) from the syntax tree of GitHub's markdown plus `wikiLinks` (an inline parser of ours, `WikiLink` / `WikiLinkMark`) and `highlights` (`==text==`, SilverBullet's: `Highlight` / `HighlightMark`): marks hidden unless the selection touches their node (ends included), never in a read-only editor; a highlight, a superscript and a subscript in their looks (`LOOKS`); a URL of its own (`https://…`, `<https://…>`; not a link's, an image's or a reference's, `URL_OWNERS`) a link, followed as the markdown link it stands for (`[url](url)`: its user's `openLink` reads links); a quote's lines `swe-quote` (its bar, its colours), its `> ` in its room, a spacer where not shown; a task's box toggles `[ ]`/`[x]` by a change of the text; a table (GitHub's, lezer's `Table`) is one block widget over its whole lines (`tableShape`: cells between its pipes, read-only, a press putting the caret at the cell's start), its markdown where the selection touches it; a fenced code block one card (`swe-code-line`, its corners `swe-code-first`/`-last`), its fences' lines unseen but their language (`swe-code-fence`, `swe-code-info`) unless the selection touches it, highlighted in its language by the names SilverBullet's editor knows (`codeLanguage` in `src/vendor.ts`, after its client/languages.ts: CodeMirror's legacy modes and its JavaScript, CSS and HTML; a tag's data block YAML), in the theme's code colours (`--editor-code-*`); an indented code block the same card; a horizontal rule a line (`swe-hr`), its `---` hidden; an embed drawn by its user's plugin (`EditorOptions.drawing`, a `FieldDrawing`: its `embed`, what goes before a link's text, `linkIcon` (a file's icon), and after it, `linkAfter` (its menu's arrow); each an `EditorEmbed`: a container given, an `AbortSignal` ended as the widget goes, an `EmbedEdit` replacing its markdown where it is now (found by its element, checked against the text it was drawn for; taken out, its line with it); `interactive`: its presses its own, an Alt+press the editor's), the page's rules of the prefixes it draws (`swa-`) put into the shadow root by `src/pagestyles.ts` (none of `swe-`, `cm-`). **The page's look** of a task's box (checked, its mark), a hashtag, a table's cells, and of what the page's editor draws of its own (headings: `swe-h1` to `swe-h6` lines, their size, weight and colour there, not on the highlight's spans; a strong text `swe-strong`; inline code `swe-inline-code`, its font and ground there; written on the line or the mark alone, what is in it keeping its own look, a hashtag's, a link's; sizes in em; a code block's lines; a rule's line: read of a sample of SilverBullet's editor's lines by `shared/silverbullet/editorsample.ts`, docs/silverware-look-fixes.md): `EditorOptions.look` (shared/editor.ts `EditorLook`, properties computed of the page's own rendering, read by the editor's user with `shared/editorlook.ts`: Silverware Layout's cell, Silverware Database's peek), written over the editor's own look by `src/look.ts` through the CSSOM; none given, its own (SilverBullet's rules do not reach the shadow root, and differ between its versions). **A list's marks and text are the editor's own rules**, as the page draws them in its editor (its rendering differs): each mark and an indent of spaces in the page's columns as SilverBullet's editor lays them out (`--editor-column`, set on its content after 2.12.1, else `1ch`: `COLUMN`, `LIST_COLUMNS`; a bullet's `- ` 2, a number's `1. ` its length and a space, a task's box `[ ] ` 4; `.swe-list-mark`, `.swe-list-indent`, `.swe-task-box`, a number right in its room), shown as written or drawn alike so the text does not move, the marks in `--editor-list-bullet-color`, a list's line from the editor's edge and its next lines after its columns (`--swe-list-hang` on `.swe-list-line`), a done task's text struck through; Silverware Layout's style page draws a cell's rendered lists by the same room. A table a card by the theme's variables (`--ui-surface-border-color`, `--ui-radius-xl`, `--ui-shadow-card`; its line only where cards have corners: 2.12.1 has the line's colour, no card), as Layout's cell draws one. **What every surface draws is compared with the page** by `ui-tests/look/surfaces.spec.ts` (docs/silverware-look-compare.md): the page, a grid's cell read and edited, a database's peek read and edited. Its lines' text is not the markdown: a test reads the editor's text from its state (`.cm-content`'s `cmTile.view.state`, the UI tests' helpers).
- **Links** (docs/silverware-editor-links.md): a link drawn as a link pressed (not its markdown shown) is its user's to follow, `EditorOptions.openLink` (its markdown; a new tab by Ctrl, Cmd or the middle button), the caret not moved; an Alt+press the editor's; none given, a press puts the caret. An escape's backslash hidden where the selection is not on it, as the page draws it.
- **Keys**: the plugin's keys first, but Esc closes a completion shown before the plugin's Esc; a key its `run` does not take goes on. `setText` is no step of the history (a plugin's text put in is no edit of the user's). The `[[` completion's pages are read again after 10 s.
- **Companions** (`COMPANION_ATTRIBUTE`, shared/editor.ts `companionBlur`): the focus gone to an element acting on the editor (Silverware Toolbar's popup) is not the editor left; told once it goes elsewhere, or to nothing (looked at every 500 ms: a companion taken away tells nothing).
- **No SilverBullet but what the init page hands over** (`listPages`, for `[[links]]` completed); its look by SilverBullet's CSS variables (the theme in `src/codemirror.ts`).

## Verified in SilverBullet (2.12.1 and main, 2026-11-14, a probe of a second CodeMirror in a grid)

- Two CodeMirrors' style classes do not collide (`style-mod` shares its counter: ours `ͼs ͼt…`, SilverBullet's `ͼ1 ͼ2…`); SilverBullet's editor looks the same after ours is made.
- Presses and keys stopped at our element (bubbling): SilverBullet's shortcuts (Ctrl+K, Ctrl+/) do not run from it, its editor is not moved, its text not changed.
- **SilverBullet takes Ctrl+Z at the window first and holds it back** (`defaultPrevented` as it reaches a later listener of the window): our undo and redo are done by a listener of ours at the window, for an event in one of our editors (`ownHistory`, shared/editor.ts; the stand-in's alike, by the browser's undo), only where the binding says the page does so (`historyAtWindow`: this library's init page, the other plugins' SilverBullet hosts in `editorsOf(…, { historyAtWindow: true })`); the shared code assumes nothing of SilverBullet.
- CodeMirror's default keys include Ctrl+/ (a comment toggled: `<!-- -->` in markdown) and Alt+A: left out.
- **SilverBullet's rules for its editor reach any CodeMirror in the page** (`#sb-main .cm-editor .cm-scroller`: 360 px padding at its bottom, among others): ours is drawn in a shadow root of its own (`EditorView`'s `root`), its element the root's host; what it inherits (font, colours, the theme's variables) still reaches it. A node in it is found by `element.shadowRoot.contains`; its selection is the root's (`shadowRoot.getSelection` in Chromium).
