# Silverware Icons: changelog

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-19)

- Panels, menus and dialogs scroll with a thin scrollbar, the same in every Silverware plugin; SilverBullet's own scrollbar is unchanged.

First versioned release.

- Lucide's icons and the emoji for the other Silverware plugins, drawn in the colors of your theme, with a picker.
- Icon sets of your own from a JavaScript file of the space.
