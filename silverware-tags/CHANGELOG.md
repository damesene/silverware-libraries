# Silverware Tags: changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

First versioned release.

- A manager of the space's tags: rename and merge them everywhere (pages, items, tasks), remove them, tag many pages at once or every page under a folder.
- Cleanup of tags likely the same, and a Tags panel in the sidebar.
- Every change reviewed first and undoable; hashtags found as SilverBullet's own parser finds them.
