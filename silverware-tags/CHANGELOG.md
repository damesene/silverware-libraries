# Silverware Tags: changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-17)

- Works with SilverBullet's next version (3.0) as well as 2.12.1: settings are saved in `Library/Config` there, and *Hide and protect meta pages* hides and protects SilverBullet's own pages (those under `Library/`) as it tells them there.
- Tags counted right on SilverBullet's next version: a paragraph's hashtags count as the page's tags, and a heading's are no longer counted twice.
- Fixed: after clicking a tag in the Tags panel, its keys (F2 among them) work on it again.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-12)

- Its menus and dialogs layered in one order with the other Silverware plugins', so none shows under another's.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

First versioned release.

- A manager of the space's tags: rename and merge them everywhere (pages, items, tasks), remove them, tag many pages at once or every page under a folder.
- Cleanup of tags likely the same, and a Tags panel in the sidebar.
- Every change reviewed first and undoable; hashtags found as SilverBullet's own parser finds them.
