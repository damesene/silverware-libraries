## Silverware Tags

A manager of the space's tags (the plan, the decisions and what was verified: [docs/silverware-tags.md](docs/silverware-tags.md)), in one window with nothing opening over it (decided 2026-10-31, after a first version of five dialogs one over another): views as tabs, *By tag* (the core's: the tags as a tree by "/", the tag picked with its name as a field renaming it, its uses, the modules' actions, its pages) and *By page* (the pages module's: pages ticked in a tree of folders, the tags they have with counts, × taking one off them all, a field adding tags to them all; Page tags opens it with the page open ticked), and a change's review in place of the view (Back / Esc to it, Apply, the option of an operation: its subtags). Beside it the **Tags panel** (the panel module, decided the same day: browsing in SilverBullet's navigator, bulk changes in the window): the module offers rows and what is done with them (`PanelOffer` in core/offers.ts: rows with the actions each offers, `can`, the actions, run, move; a click opens a level only, `"navigator:expand"`; a row's actions are every module's `panelActions` (core/module.ts `PanelAction`: icon, label, key, order, when, run; the panel's own +, − and Open, the rename module's Rename with F2), which the library page's Lua makes the view's `actions` and `keymap` of, knowing none of them) and knows nothing of who draws them; the library page's Lua makes SilverBullet's view of them (`view.define`, in 2.12.1: verified in its source, tag `2.12.1` of 2026-10-12; `presentation.mode = "tree"`, a count as a decoration, segments, `keymap` F2, `onMove`), only where `view.define` exists; the playground draws them as a list. Verified in the local SilverBullet (main, 2026-10-16) by a script: the tree, a click opening the window at the tag, F2 (the navigator focuses its own filter field after the key is handled: the window takes the focus back for a moment, `keepFocus`), a drag onto a level reviewing the rename. The built-in space tree cannot be changed (`register` refuses a built-in's name), and the navigator has no selection of several rows: why bulk changes stay in the window. **Hide and protect meta pages** (`silverware.tags.protectMeta`, on by default, decided 2026-10-31): a meta page (its own tag `meta` or under it) is out of the catalog altogether (`catalogOf`'s `hideMeta`: its tags show nowhere, it is in no operation's pages) and a plan's guard leaves one alone as read (`Guard` in core/plan.ts: the index may not have caught up), so renaming `#auto` renames it everywhere but on meta pages. Off: SilverBullet's own tags (`meta` and under it: `isMetaTag` in tags/names.ts, as its plug-api/lib/tags.ts) are apart as its tree has meta pages apart (its space tree's segments in 2.12.1: All and Pages leave meta pages out, Meta shows them alone): the panel's segments All, Page tags, Tasks without them and Meta with them alone, the window's Tags | Meta, By page without meta pages (`metaPages` in core/catalog.ts) nor their meta tags to take off (verified in the local SilverBullet the same day). Modules use each other's only through offers, as Silverware Table's: each module gets its own host (`Controller.hostFor`), `offer(api)` keeps what it offers under its name, `offered(module)` asks for a module's (the contracts in core/offers.ts: the panel's drag through the rename module's). Page tags are written into the frontmatter in the page's own form; a rename changes every hashtag (items, tasks, tables, headings too). Every change across pages is planned (pages read and edited, nothing written), previewed (lines before and after, pages left alone and why), applied page by page where a page is still as read, and kept for Undo (an operation as any other, `undoOperation` in core/plan.ts: reviewed in the window with its progress, each page written back where it is still as applied; applied, its change goes off the history, `undoes`). All of it is the page writer's (shared/pages/, since step 2b of docs/silverware-database.md): an operation says what is to change on each page in the writer's terms (`change(p, page)`: `p.tags.rename`, `merge`, `remove`, `add`; a custom operation's text edit through `p.editText`, the one way past the model), the writer plans, writes and undoes; no part of Silverware Tags writes a page's text (isolation.test.ts). Built as the others: a core that knows no module, modules turned off not loaded (`silverware.tags.modules.<name>.enabled`), and SilverBullet behind a `Space` of its own.
